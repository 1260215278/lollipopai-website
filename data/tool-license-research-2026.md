## 2026 TOS Changes That Matter for Short Drama

1. **Suno (Sept 3, 2026 — Major restriction)** — TOS revised Aug 10, effective Sept 3. Commercial use now requires a paid-plan **download** through Suno's allocation system. You can no longer monetize songs you generate but don't download. Free-tier and trial downloads remain non-commercial. [Source: Veena audit](https://www.veena.studio/blog/suno-commercial-use-rules); [Suno ToS revision date confirmed](https://hypeart.ai/glossary/suno-ai-song-generator/)

2. **Udio (Nov 12, 2025, still in effect 2026)** — No download allowed on any plan, no commercial use on any plan. This was a silent transition after UMG litigation; UMG partnership (Feb 2026) made no TOS change. Short drama producers should avoid Udio entirely in 2026. [Source: aialleyway.com](https://aialleyway.com/can-you-sell-suno-music/)

3. **OpenAI Sora 2 (Apr 26, 2026 — restriction)** — Sora video generation was pulled from ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscriptions. Sora 2 is now **API-only** ($0.10/sec basic, $0.30/sec Pro). Ownership remains clean. [Source: hypeart.ai pricing](https://hypeart.ai/pricing/sora-ai-video-generator-pricing/)

4. **Kling AI (Apr 21, 2026 — clarification)** — Added explicit distinction between free and paid tier commercial rights in revised TOS §4.6. Paid membership "is" your written commercial-use permission.

5. **Runway (Oct 23, 2026)** — TOS updated; retained free-tier commercial use. No change to ownership or usage rights.

6. **ElevenLabs Prohibited Use Policy (Aug 17, 2026)** — Updated; no new commercial restrictions but reaffirmed consent requirements for voice cloning and Film/TV/Studio Games carve-out in Music.

## Critical Questions for Short Drama Producers

### When do you need the ElevenLabs "Commercial Voice Cloning" license?
You do **NOT** need a separate "commercial voice cloning" license — the commercial rights are granted by your paid plan. What you DO need:
- **Instant Voice Cloning** (Starter plan, $6/mo): Can clone ANY voice, but you **must obtain written consent** from the voice owner. ElevenLabs will ban accounts for cloning without consent.
- **Professional Voice Cloning** (Creator plan, $22/mo+): Can ONLY clone your own voice (verified via account ownership + audio proof). Cannot clone others' voices even with consent, per ElevenLabs policy.
- **Commercial Voice Library**: ElevenLabs offers a marketplace of pre-cloned voices for commercial use — these DO have explicit commercial rights bundled.
- All paid plans include commercial rights for output IF you hold proper rights to the voice being cloned.

**Source**: [ElevenLabs Voice Cloning overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning); [Prohibited Use Policy](https://elevenlabs.io/ko/use-policy); [MargaBagus analysis](https://margabagus.com/elevenlabs-voice-consent-policy/)

### Stock music for AI short dramas — Epidemic Sound vs Artlist?
Both offer universal sync licenses for commercial use. Key difference:
- **Epidemic Sound Creator** ($15/mo): Includes AI voice generation with 250K credits/mo. Sublicensable for client work.
- **Artlist AI Suite Pro** ($16/mo): Includes AI voiceover generation (Pro plan). License NOT sublicensable to client in a different project.
- **Neither Epidemic nor Artlist claims ownership** of your final video — they license the music/sfxx component only.

**Source**: [Epidemic Creator plan](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/creator-plan/); [Artlist client coverage](https://artlist.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6094395287581-Client-projects-and-coverage)
